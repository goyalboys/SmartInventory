# Order Cancellation Policy

## Overview

You can cancel an order depending on its current status and payment method.

## When Cancellation Is Allowed

| Order Status   | Customer Can Cancel? | Notes                                      |
|----------------|----------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| pending        | Yes                  | Full cancellation, stock restored          |
| confirmed      | Yes                  | May be subject to merchant review          |
| shipped        | No                   | Request return after delivery instead      |
| delivered      | No                   | Use return/refund policy                   |
| cancelled      | N/A                  | Order already cancelled                    |

## Prepaid Orders

If you cancel a **paid** prepaid order before shipping, a full refund is issued automatically to your original payment method.

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD orders can be cancelled freely while status is **pending** or **confirmed**. No payment has been collected.

## How to Cancel

- Use the chat assistant: "Cancel order [ORDER_ID]"
- Cancellation requires confirmation before it is executed
- You will receive a notification when cancellation completes

## Merchant Cancellations

Merchants may cancel orders due to stock issues or fulfillment problems. In such cases, prepaid customers receive a full refund.
