# Shipping Policy

## Overview

Orders are shipped by merchants through SmartInventory partner couriers.

## Processing Time

- Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days after confirmation
- Processing may take longer during sale periods or for made-to-order items

## Delivery Timeline

| Region        | Estimated Delivery |
|---------------|--------------------|
| Metro cities  | 2–4 business days  |
| Tier 2 cities | 4–7 business days  |
| Remote areas  | 7–10 business days |

## Shipping Charges

- Free shipping on orders above ₹499 (merchant-dependent)
- Standard shipping fee of ₹49–₹99 may apply below threshold

## Order Tracking

- Order status updates: pending → confirmed → shipped → delivered
- Tracking details are shared when the order is marked **shipped**
- Check order status in your account or ask the chat assistant

## Delivery Issues

- If delivery fails, the courier attempts redelivery up to 2 times
- Undelivered packages may be returned to the merchant after 7 days
- Contact support for lost or delayed shipments after the estimated window
