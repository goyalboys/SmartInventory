# Damaged Product Policy

## Overview

If your product arrives damaged, defective, or broken, SmartInventory will help you get a refund or replacement.

## What Qualifies as Damaged

- Visible physical damage to the product or packaging
- Product does not work as described due to manufacturing defect
- Missing essential parts or accessories
- Leakage or spoilage (for applicable categories)

## What to Do Immediately

1. **Do not discard** the product or outer packaging
2. Take clear photos: product damage, packaging, shipping label
3. Report within **48 hours** of delivery for fastest resolution
4. Provide your order ID when reporting

## Resolution Options

You may choose (subject to availability):

- **Full refund** — for irreparable damage or if you prefer not to wait
- **Replacement** — same product shipped again at no extra cost
- **Partial refund** — for minor cosmetic damage you agree to keep

## Inspection

Our support team may request additional photos or a brief video. For high-value items, courier pickup for inspection may be arranged.

## Timeline

- Initial response within 24 hours
- Refund or replacement initiated within 3–5 business days after approval
