# Warranty Policy

## Overview

Warranty coverage depends on the product category and manufacturer.

## Standard Warranty

- **Electronics:** 1-year manufacturer warranty unless stated otherwise on the product page
- **Appliances:** 1–2 years per brand documentation
- **Other categories:** No warranty unless explicitly listed

## What Warranty Covers

- Manufacturing defects
- Functional failures under normal use
- Not covered: physical damage, water damage, unauthorized repairs, normal wear

## Warranty Claims

1. Contact support with order ID, product serial number, and issue description
2. Provide proof of purchase
3. Manufacturer or authorized service center may inspect the product
4. Repair, replacement, or store credit per manufacturer terms

## SmartInventory Role

SmartInventory facilitates warranty claims but final resolution follows the manufacturer's policy. We help coordinate between customer and merchant/manufacturer.
