# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**Q: How do I track my order?**  
A: Ask the chat assistant "Where is my order?" or check order status in your account. Status flows: pending → confirmed → shipped → delivered.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**  
A: Address changes are only possible while the order is **pending**. Contact support immediately.

**Q: How long until my order ships?**  
A: Usually 1–2 business days after confirmation. See the shipping policy for delivery estimates.

## Cancellations & Refunds

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**  
A: Yes, if status is pending or confirmed. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled — request a return after delivery.

**Q: When will I get my refund?**  
A: Prepaid refunds take 5–10 business days to appear in your account after approval. COD refunds via bank transfer within 7 business days.

## Returns & Damaged Items

**Q: My product arrived damaged. What should I do?**  
A: Report within 48 hours with photos. You can choose a refund or replacement. See the damaged product policy.

**Q: What is the return window?**  
A: 7 days from delivery for most products. Some categories are non-returnable.

## Payments

**Q: Is Cash on Delivery available?**  
A: Yes, where the merchant supports it. You pay when the order is delivered.

**Q: My payment failed. Was I charged?**  
A: Failed Razorpay payments do not create a confirmed order. Any temporary hold is released by your bank.

## Support

**Q: How do I contact support?**  
A: Use this chat assistant or create a support ticket. Include your order ID for faster help.
