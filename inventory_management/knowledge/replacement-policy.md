# Replacement Policy

## Overview

Replacements are offered when a product is defective, damaged in transit, or the wrong item was delivered.

## Eligibility

- Report within 48 hours of delivery (damaged/wrong item)
- Product must be returned in received condition with packaging
- Same product variant must be in stock for direct replacement

## Replacement Process

1. Report the issue with order ID and photos
2. Support approves replacement or offers refund alternative
3. Return pickup arranged (if required)
4. Replacement shipped within 3–5 business days after approval
5. No additional shipping charge for eligible replacements

## If Product Is Out of Stock

If the same item is unavailable:

- Full refund offered
- Or substitute product with customer consent
- Or store credit with optional bonus (merchant discretion)

## Exchanges for Size/Color

Size/color exchanges follow the return policy: return original item, place new order. Some merchants offer direct exchange — check product page.
