# Refund Policy

## Overview

SmartInventory offers refunds under specific conditions. This policy explains when you are eligible and how refunds are processed.

## Eligibility

You may request a refund if:

- The product arrived damaged or defective
- You received the wrong item
- The order was cancelled before shipping (prepaid orders)
- Delivery failed and the item was returned to the merchant

Refunds are **not** available for:

- Change of mind after delivery (unless return policy applies)
- Products marked as final sale
- Digital goods after download/access

## Refund Timeline

- **Prepaid (Razorpay):** Refunds are initiated within 3–5 business days after approval. Amount appears in your account within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Refunds for eligible COD orders are processed via bank transfer or store credit within 7 business days.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Contact support via the chat assistant or raise a support ticket
2. Provide your order ID and reason for refund
3. For damaged items, photos may be required
4. Our team reviews within 48 hours

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may apply if only some items in a multi-item order are eligible.
